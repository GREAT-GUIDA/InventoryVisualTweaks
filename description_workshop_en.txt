All effects are fully toggleable and adjustable. If there's some detail you want to adjust but can't, let me know and I'll add it.

Item border colors and styles can be adjusted in settings and also adapt to texture packs.

And I did reference some of those effects. But I made this mod mainly because I don't want to enable a bunch of scattered visual mods at once, and they don't look unified when stacked together.

Many of them also add unnecessary stuff. Some aren't purely client-side, some lack configuration, and Naka's Inventory Tweaks even crashes on me every time I reload mods.

So I made an integrated item visual tweaks mod to my own taste.

Actually, I have a larger-scale visual tweaks mod, but that one might still take a long time to finish, so I'm releasing this mod first.
